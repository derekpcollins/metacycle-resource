Three options:

1. **Replace the alarm module.** It's under the seat, and a new one comes with two key fobs.
2. **Clone a fob.** If you still have one, a fob cloner can copy its lock and unlock signals. Proximity unlocking probably won't work.
3. **Remove the alarm** and use a key switch instead. See [Alarm removal & bypass](/guides/alarm-removal).
