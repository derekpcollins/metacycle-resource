Drive and reverse work, but the horn, turn signals and lights don't. The controller hasn't started correctly, and the bike may have been started while charging. Reset it:

1. Disconnect the charger from the bike and the wall.
2. Turn off the Metacycle and arm the alarm.
3. Wait one minute for the systems to power down.
4. Connect the charger to the bike.
5. Plug the charger into the outlet.
6. Wait a minute. You might hear a click.
7. Unplug the charger from the wall and wait for a click and for the LED to go off.
8. Disconnect the charger from the Metacycle.
9. Disarm the alarm and start the Metacycle normally.
