---
symptom: Cables rubbing the rear tire
system: electrical
likely_causes:
  - Tight clearance lets the orange power wire touch the rear tire.
  - The brake-light wires sag onto the rear tire.
checks:
  - step: Look along the swingarm for the orange power wire and the brake-light wires, and check whether either can touch the tire.
    pass: They're clear of the tire. Recheck after your next ride.
    fail: A wire touches or rubs the tire. Zip-tie it to the swingarm.
    most_likely: true
fix:
  - note: Secure the orange power wire and brake-light wires to the swingarm with zip ties.
  - part: swingarm
confidence: community-reported
sources:
  - https://metacyclopedia.github.io/problems/
---

The clearance is tight and the routing questionable, so the orange power wire or the brake-light wires can rub on the rear tire. Metacyclopedia's [Problems page](https://metacyclopedia.github.io/problems/) has photos of the wire routing from the side and from above.
