Some controllers won't save sport-mode current, busbar amps, brand or other settings. The fix in [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6rSq_TIzTU) writes various settings and flashes two different firmware versions. Metacyclopedia reports it has worked on a Metacycle controller. Save a backup of your settings first.
