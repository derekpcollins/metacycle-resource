With the factory setting the display reads about 80 mph at a true 70 mph. Owners get accurate readings after setting the circumference to 1675 mm. You only need the two buttons on the back of the display ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vdKBwa3N2Kw)):

1. Hold the **left** button to enter programming mode. The screen shows "CC".
2. Enter **153**: tap the left button to raise the blinking digit, and the right button to move one digit right.
3. Set the circumference to **1675**. Leave the pole pairs and offset alone, or the bike may stop working.
4. Long-press the **right** button to exit.

Other codes in the same menu: 974 switches between km/h and mph; 372 resets the odometer (only three times).
