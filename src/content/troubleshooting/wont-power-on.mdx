---
symptom: Won't power on / remotes dead
system: electrical
checks:
  - step: Make sure the engine kill switch is set to "On".
    pass: It's on. Continue.
    fail: Set it to "On" and try again.
  - step: Wait a while. If the controller or motor got too hot, everything shuts down as a safety measure.
    pass: Still dead after cooling. Continue.
    fail: It powers on once cool. Heat was the cause; ease off until you know why it overheated.
  - step: Use the activation dongle, or plug in the charger, so the battery connects internally. Listen for a click.
    pass: Still dead. Continue.
    fail: It powers on. The battery needed activating.
  - step: Replace the batteries in the key fobs, and check the fob LEDs blink when you press a button (that means they're transmitting).
    pass: Fobs blink but the bike still doesn't respond. Continue.
    fail: The bike responds with new batteries. Done.
  - step: Check the 5 A blade fuse in the battery compartment.
    pass: The fuse is good. Continue.
    fail: It's burned out. Replace it.
  - step: If the alarm still doesn't chirp when you press the fob buttons, unplug it and bridge the harness's red/white and yellow wires (the alarm's red and orange) under the seat.
    pass: The bike turns on. The alarm is the fault; replace or bypass it.
    fail: The bike stays off. The problem isn't the alarm. TODO, next step not documented; ask in the community groups.
    most_likely: true
fix:
  - part: fuse-5a
    note: If the fuse was blown.
  - part: battery-activator
    note: To wake the battery after a reconnect (or plug in the charger).
  - guide: alarm-removal
    note: If bridging the wires turns the bike on — replace or bypass the alarm.
  - part: alarm-module
confidence: community-reported
sources:
  - https://metacyclopedia.github.io/problems/
  - https://metacyclopedia.github.io/battery/
  - https://metacyclopedia.github.io/removing-alarm-system/
  - https://metacyclopedia.github.io/battery-activator/
---

Work through the checks in order. The check marked _most likely_ is the alarm module, which Metacyclopedia calls extremely troublesome and a frequent source of problems.
