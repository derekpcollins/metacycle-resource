---
symptom: Won't power on / remotes dead
system: electrical
likely_causes:
  - Kill switch is in the off position.
  - Controller or motor is too hot.
  - Battery needs reactivating.
  - Key fob batteries are dead.
  - Blown 5 A fuse.
  - Alarm module fault.
checks:
  - step: Check the kill switch.
    pass: Switch is on. Continue.
    fail: Flip it on and try again.
  - step: Let the controller and motor cool down, then retry.
    pass: Still dead. Continue.
    fail: Powers on. Heat was the cause.
  - step: Reactivate the battery (activator dongle or charger).
    pass: Still dead. Continue.
    fail: Powers on. Battery needed reactivating.
  - step: Replace the key fob batteries.
    pass: Still dead. Continue.
    fail: Remotes work again.
  - step: Check the 5 A fuse.
    pass: Fuse is good. Continue.
    fail: Replace the fuse.
  - step: If the alarm is silent, bridge the orange and red wires under the seat to test.
    pass: Bike powers on. The alarm is the likely fault.
    fail: TODO — next diagnostic step not yet documented.
fix:
  - note: Work through the checks in order. If the alarm is at fault, see "Lost key fobs" for alarm replacement or removal options (TODO guide).
confidence: community-reported
sources:
  - https://metacyclopedia.github.io/problems/
---

Work through the checks in order; most owners find the cause in the first few.
