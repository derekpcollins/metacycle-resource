The stock seat is a common complaint. Two makers offer more comfortable seats: [Corbin-Pacific](https://www.corbin.com/sondors/sonmeta.shtml) and [Tapia's Seats](https://www.tapiasseats.com/shop) (custom). Owners report Tapia's installs much like the Corbin.

<Step n={1}>
  Remove the stock seat: undo the two M6 × 1.00 × 25 bolts with a 5 mm Allen key, then pull up on
  the front while sliding the seat forward.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Fit the new seat. Corbin's installation is shown step by step in [this owner
  video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJrRIZGzl2A). TODO: written steps for each seat aren't
  documented yet. Share yours with photos.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>Refit and tighten the bolts. TODO: seat bolt torque isn't documented.</Step>
