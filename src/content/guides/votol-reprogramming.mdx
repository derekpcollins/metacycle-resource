The controller talks [CAN bus](https://metacyclopedia.github.io/can-bus/) to the bike. A USB adapter turns that into a serial link for Votol's Windows software. Other routes exist: a cable to the 20-pin connector behind the display, Votol's Bluetooth adapter and Android app, or [MetaHack](https://metacyclopedia.github.io/metahack/) (an app plus a custom adapter). This guide covers the most common route: the USB port in the storage compartment.

### Check your bike and build the cable

<Step n={1}>
  Check the USB port can carry data. Take off the seat and carefully open the sheathing on the cable
  going to the storage-compartment USB port. **Four wires:** this method should work. **Two wires:**
  it's power only, so use the [20-pin
  method](https://metacyclopedia.github.io/connect-with-20-pin-cable/) instead.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Get the Votol EM200-2SP USB programming cable: it's **blue with a black band**. Ones without the
  band are the wrong version.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Take any USB-A cable with four wires (a data cable, not charge-only). Cut off the other end and
  strip the outer sheath.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Wire it to the adapter. Only H (Data +) and L (Data −) are used:
  - USB green (Data +) → adapter **H** (middle, pin 3)
  - USB white (Data −) → adapter **L** (left of centre, pin 4)
  - USB red (+5 V) and black (GND): unused. Tape each one off so it can't touch anything.

The pins are labeled on the bottom of the adapter.

</Step>

### Connect and back up

<Step n={5}>
  Install the Votol EM V3-40 software on a Windows PC. No driver is needed on Windows 10; the
  default Microsoft driver works and the adapter appears as a COM port.
</Step>

<Step n={6}>
  Turn on the Metacycle and plug the cable into the storage-compartment USB port. Don't connect it
  to the computer yet.
</Step>

<Step n={7}>Launch the software and note any ports already listed in the PORT drop-down.</Step>

<Step n={8}>
  Plug the adapter into the computer and press **Research**. Select the new COM port, set the baud
  rate to **115200**, and make sure **CAN enable is not checked**.
</Step>

<Step n={9}>
  Press **Open**, then **Connect**. The software reads the controller and fills in its pages.
</Step>

<Step n={10}>
  **Save Param** first, before changing anything, and keep that file safe. If you never made a
  backup, Metacyclopedia shares a factory 2021 Metacycle settings file (made with EM V3-40).
</Step>

### Change settings

<Step n={11}>
  Change one thing at a time and press **Param Write** to commit it. Settings owners have shared
  (all at their own risk) are on Metacyclopedia's [Programming
  page](https://metacyclopedia.github.io/programming/). The factory limits are under 200 A (220 A in
  sport mode).
</Step>

<Step n={12}>
  Test ride gently and check for heat. If anything behaves oddly, write your backup back.
</Step>

<Step n={13}>
  If sport-mode current, busbar amps or other settings won't save (they read back unchanged), the
  controller is read-only. Follow the video [VOTOL EM260/2: fixing read-only busbar / sport
  current](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6rSq_TIzTU), which writes settings and flashes two
  firmware versions. Metacyclopedia reports it has worked on a Metacycle controller.
</Step>
