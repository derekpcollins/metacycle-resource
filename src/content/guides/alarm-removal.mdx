The alarm module can fail without warning and its key fobs' batteries die. A former Sondors engineer called it one of the bike's worst features.

Wire colours differ on each side of the plug:

- Alarm **red** = harness **red with white stripe**
- Alarm **orange** = harness **yellow**
- Alarm **pink** = harness **blue with white stripe**

### Confirm it's the alarm

<Step n={1}>
  Take off the seat: undo the two bolts (5 mm Allen), then pull up on the front while sliding it
  forward.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>Find the alarm module under the seat.</Step>

<Step n={3}>Follow its wires toward the front of the bike and unplug the alarm module.</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  On the **harness** side, bridge the red/white wire and the yellow wire with a spare piece of wire
  or a paperclip. **Bike turns on:** the alarm is the likely fault. **Bike stays off:** the problem
  isn't the alarm; go back to [Won't power on](/troubleshooting/wont-power-on).
</Step>

<Step n={5}>
  Reconnect the alarm to make sure it still doesn't work. If the bike now works with the alarm
  connected, the cause may have been excessive heat.
</Step>

### Option A: replace the module

<Step n={6}>
  Order a [replacement alarm module](/parts/alarm-module) first (it comes with two fobs). The old
  module is held on with sticky tape: unplug it from the harness, then pull hard to pry it off.
</Step>

<Step n={7}>
  Keep the old unit until the new one works. Replacements sometimes come with a different plug, so
  you may need to splice the new unit's wires onto the old plug.
</Step>

### Option B: replace + switchable bypass

<Step n={8}>
  One owner fitted a three-position key switch: one position keeps the alarm working, another
  bypasses it. This needs drilling. Follow [the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ps6psLgywKc).
  Bridging to the alarm's pink wire (harness blue/white) as well also lights the power button.
</Step>

### Option C: total bypass with a key switch

<Step n={9}>
  Wire a [key switch](/parts/key-switch) between the alarm's orange (harness yellow) and red
  (harness red/white) wires. A 20 mm diameter switch fits without drilling.
</Step>

<Step n={10}>
  To avoid cutting the bike's harness, wire the switch to a spare [DJ7065F-2.2-11
  plug](/parts/alarm-connector) and plug that in where the alarm was.
</Step>

<Step n={11}>Insulate every connection, refit the seat and test the switch.</Step>
