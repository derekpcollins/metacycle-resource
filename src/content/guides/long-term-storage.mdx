The alarm module draws power all the time, so a bike left with its battery connected slowly drains it.

<Step n={1}>
  Disconnect the battery before storage so the alarm can't drain it. To take it out completely (for
  example, to keep it indoors over winter), follow [Battery removal &
  reinstall](/guides/battery-removal).
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  To top it up while it's out of the bike, use the charge-adapter cable that came with the bike:
  attach it to the battery, then plug in the charger. See [Cold-weather
  charging](/guides/cold-weather-charging) for the full charging procedure.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Don't charge near or below freezing. TODO: the ideal storage charge level and storage temperature
  range aren't sourced yet. Help wanted.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  When you reconnect the battery, wake it up with the [battery activator](/parts/battery-activator)
  or by plugging in the [charger](/parts/charger). Listen for the click.
</Step>
