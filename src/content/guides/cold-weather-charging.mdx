The stock charger fills the 4 kWh pack in about 5 hours. The one firm rule for cold weather: **never charge a battery that's near or below freezing**.

<Step n={1}>
  Check the battery's temperature first. If the bike or battery has been somewhere at or below
  freezing, don't charge yet. TODO: how long to let a cold pack warm up, and whether the BMS blocks
  cold charging, aren't documented. Share what you know.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  If you're charging the battery out of the bike, attach the charge-adapter cord to the battery.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>Plug the cable with the blue end into the Metacycle (or into the adapter cord).</Step>

<Step n={4}>Fit the right mains plug to the charger (US NEMA 5-15 or EU Type C).</Step>

<Step n={5}>
  Plug the charger into the wall. If its light blinks red-red-green, that's a communication error,
  but it still charges.
</Step>

<Step n={6}>When charging is done, unplug the charger from the wall outlet first.</Step>

<Step n={7}>
  Wait for the click from the Metacycle (it should come fairly soon), then disconnect the charger
  from the bike or the battery adapter.
</Step>
