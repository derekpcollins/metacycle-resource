The inspection half of this guide comes straight from the Sondors owner's manual (§17, §20 and §21.1). The replacement half is thin: nobody has written up the Metacycle procedure yet, and axle torque values aren't documented anywhere. If you've done it, please [share your steps](/contribute/form?type=fix&system=wheels-tires&page=/guides/tire-inspection-replacement).

### How often

| When                        | What to check                                             |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Before every ride           | Pressure, tread depth, visible damage                     |
| Every 500 mi or 1 month     | Full inspection below, plus wheels for run-out and damage |
| Every 6,000 mi or 18 months | Full tire inspection                                      |

The manual's maintenance table also lists wheel run-out at 3,000 mi / 12 months.

### Inspection

<Step n={1}>
  Check pressure with the tires **cold**, meaning not ridden for at least 3 hours. Set the front to
  **32 psi (221 kPa)** and the rear to **30 psi (207 kPa)**. Put the valve stem caps back on
  afterwards. (The maintenance table says 32 psi for both; the manual's tire page and Metacyclopedia
  both give 30 psi for the rear.)
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Measure tread depth. The manual gives two limits: the maintenance table says replace below **2
  mm**, and the tire page says below **1.6 mm (1/16 in)**. Plan to replace at 2 mm.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Inspect both tires for cuts, cracks, splits or missing tread lugs in the tread or sidewall; bumps
  or bulges in the tire body; and exposed thread or cords.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Look for uneven wear. The manual says wear on one side of the tread, or flat spots, means there's
  a problem with the tire or with the bike. Find out which before you fit a new tire.
</Step>

<Step n={5}>
  Inspect both wheels for bends, cracks and impact marks. Spin each wheel and check for run-out
  (wobble).
</Step>

<Step n={6}>
  On the rear, check that no cables are touching the tire. The orange power wire and the brake-light
  wires are known to rub; see [Cables rubbing rear tire](/troubleshooting/cables-rubbing-rear-tire).
</Step>

If anything in steps 2–5 fails, the manual says to replace the wheel and/or tire immediately.

### Replacement

<Step n={7}>
  Buy the right size: front **110/70 R17**, rear **150/60 R17**. Bikes shipped with Michelin Pilot
  Road 5 or CST CM-NK01 tires (possibly Kenda on some). The manual says to use only "approved radial
  tires"; with Sondors gone there's no approved list, so match the size and type of what you have.
  See [Tires](/parts/tires).
</Step>

<Step n={8}>
  Check whether you have tubeless tires: if the spokes go to tabs on the wheel rim, they're
  tubeless.
</Step>

<Step n={9}>
  Remove the wheel and change the tire. Owner videos: [short
  version](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7U2baauyt8) and [long
  version](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VoAgnKyHUw4). TODO: a written procedure for each wheel.
  The rear wheel is the [hub motor](/parts/hub-motor), so it's heavier than the front and its wiring
  is attached to it; how to disconnect it safely isn't documented yet.
</Step>

<Step n={10}>
  Reinstall the wheel. TODO: axle nut and wheel bolt torque values aren't documented anywhere yet.
  If you know them (with a source or a photo of the manual), please [share
  them](/contribute/form?type=fix&system=wheels-tires&page=/guides/tire-inspection-replacement).
</Step>

<Step n={11}>
  Check that the front wheel bolts, rear wheel bolts and swingarm bolt are secure. The factory
  marked them with blue paint: the mark on each bolt should line up with the mark on its housing.
</Step>

<Step n={12}>
  Set the pressures (step 1), then check the speedometer on your first ride. A different tire brand
  or profile can change the rolling circumference. The fix is the display's circumference setting;
  see [Speedometer reads wrong](/troubleshooting/speedometer-reads-wrong).
</Step>
