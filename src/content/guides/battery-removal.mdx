The "removable" battery is really a bolted-in pack. Removal takes a T40 and a T50 Torx. Owner video: [Sondors MetaCycle battery removal and replace](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rWQAdfNzzi8).

### Removal

<Step n={1}>
  On the side **without** the charging port, remove the two T40 bolts from the battery cover.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>From the bottom, pull the cover upwards, then pull it out to remove the shield.</Step>

<Step n={3}>Remove the two T50 bolts from the battery brackets, and remove the brackets.</Step>

<Step n={4}>Disconnect the orange wire and nudge the battery out.</Step>

<Step n={5}>Disconnect the black wire and nudge the battery out.</Step>

<Step n={6}>Disconnect the harness.</Step>

<Step n={7}>
  Remove the battery. The battery compartment also hides the 5 A blade fuse, so this is a good time
  to check it.
</Step>

### Reinstall

<Step n={8}>
  Reverse steps 1–7. Slide the battery in carefully: it can tilt or jam on the way in.
</Step>

<Step n={9}>
  Wake the battery up: connect the [battery activator](/parts/battery-activator) or plug in the
  [charger](/parts/charger). This powers the internal relay (solenoid) so the battery can feed the
  controller and motor. You'll hear a click inside the bike.
</Step>
